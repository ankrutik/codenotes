- #subtitles #ffmpeg To shift time signatures in .srt subtitle file
	- ```bash
	  ffmpeg -itsoffset -20 -i 'Jurassic Park (1993) {edition-35mm Scan}.srt' -c copy output.srt
	  ```
	- Here `-20` denotes hastening subtitles by 20 seconds. `itsoffset` will input the delay.
